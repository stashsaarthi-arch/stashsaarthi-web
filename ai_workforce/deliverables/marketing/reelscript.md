## ⏸️ Clipchamp Pause & Punctuation Secret Formula

Microsoft Clipchamp ki AI aawaz me exact pauses kaise lagayein:

1. **Short Breath Pause (~0.3s to 0.5s):** Use a single comma `,` (e.g. `Bhai sach bata raha hoon, [0.4s breath pause]`).
2. **Dramatic / Suspense Pause (~0.7s to 1.0s):** Use triple dots `...` (e.g. `Yaar ek honest sawaal hai... [0.8s suspense pause]`).
3. **Thought Pivot / Transition Pause (~0.5s to 0.6s):** Use an em-dash `—` (e.g. `verified senior citizen ke ghar bag lock karo — [0.5s pivot pause]`).
4. **Full Sentence Stop (~0.6s to 0.8s):** Use a full period `.` or exclamation `!`.
5. **Major Section Break (~1.0s to 1.2s):** Ek blank line chhod kar naye paragraph se shuru karein.
