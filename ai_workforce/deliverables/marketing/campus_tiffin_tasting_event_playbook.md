# Saarthi Kitchen: "Ghar Ka Khana" Campus Tasting Activation Playbook

**Target Audience:** Students tired of oily mess food near CSJMU, IIT Kanpur & Kakadeo PGs  
**Initiative:** 100-Pack Free Senior Host Tiffin Sampler & Pop-Up Table  
**Unit Economics:** ₹90/meal (Host earns ₹55/meal, Platform net margin ₹16/meal)  

---

## 1. Event Concept: "Hostel Mess vs. Dadi Ki Rasoi"
Set up a pop-up tasting table 50 meters outside CSJMU Gate 2 / IITK Gate with two trays:
- **Tray A (Mess Food):** Watery dal, rubbery roti.
- **Tray B (StashSaarthi Host Tiffin):** Fresh phulkas with desi ghee, paneer bhurji, yellow dal tadka, homemade pickle.

---

## 2. On-Ground Conversion Flow
1. **Taste & Blind Vote:** Students sample Tray B and scan a QR code to vote on taste.
2. **Instant Subscription Offer:** "First 3 Tiffins at ₹49/meal" on signing up for monthly meal passes.
3. **WhatsApp Instant Daily Menu Push:** Added to daily tiffin dispatch broadcast for automated morning menu selections.
