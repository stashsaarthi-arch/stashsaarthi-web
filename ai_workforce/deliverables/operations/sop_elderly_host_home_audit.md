# SOP-OPS-01: Senior Host Home Visit, Safety Audit & Verification Protocol

**Document ID:** SS-OPS-SOP-001  
**Version:** 2.4 (Kanpur Cluster Rollout)  
**Target Areas:** Kalyanpur (CSJMU/IITK), Kakadeo, Rawatpur, Nawabganj  
**Responsible Role:** Campus Operations Concierge & City Lead  

---

## 1. Objective
Establish an uncompromising, 4-tier physical and document verification audit for every prospective senior host before any student luggage or co-living listing goes live on StashSaarthi.

---

## 2. Pre-Visit Stage (Digital Screen)
1. **Aadhaar & Utility Bill Matching:** Name on electricity/water bill must match Aadhaar card or registered ownership deed.
2. **Emergency Contact Verification:** Verify at least 1 working primary family contact (son/daughter/local guardian) with phone OTP check.
3. **Proximity Radius Check:** Location must be ≤ 1.5 km from the designated campus gate (e.g., IITK Hall 13 Gate, CSJMU Main Gate).

---

## 3. Physical Home Audit Checklist (In-Person Concierge Visit)

### 3.1 Space & Storage Node Integrity
- [ ] **Dry & Pest-Free Verification:** Ensure dedicated corner/cupboard/room is 100% moisture-free, raised above floor level (wooden pallet/rack provided by StashSaarthi).
- [ ] **Capacity Tagging:** Measure dimensions and assign maximum bag capacity (`MaxBags = FloorAreaSqFt / 1.5`).
- [ ] **Access Control:** Storage area must be indoors behind locked outer doors.

### 3.2 Safety & Senior Friendly Amenities
- [ ] **Emergency SOS Setup:** Test the 1-touch WhatsApp Concierge shortcut on host's smartphone.
- [ ] **House Norms Signage:** Fix the official StashSaarthi Host House Norms Board in the foyer (Curfew, Visitor Policy, Kitchen Norms).
- [ ] **Welcome Kit Handover:** Provide 10x Tamper-Evident Laser Barcode Seals, Welcome Guide, and Escrow Passbook.

---

## 4. Host Legal & Financial Activation
1. **Waiver & Liability Charter:** Host signs the zero-intrusion, ₹10,000 platform indemnification bond.
2. **Payout Bank Account / UPI Setup:** Verify bank account name matches Aadhaar for Tuesday automated escrow disbursement.
3. **Badge Issuance:** Host profile marked with **"🛡️ StashSaarthi Verified Gold Host"** badge on the platform.

---

## 5. Escalation Matrix & Periodic Re-Audit
- **Quarterly Random Audit:** Unannounced audit every 90 days.
- **Incident Escalation:** Any safety flag triggers immediate node suspension within 15 minutes and physical concierge dispatch within 30 minutes.
