# SOP-OPS-04: Saarthi Kitchen Senior Host Culinary & Food Safety Standards

**Document ID:** SS-OPS-SOP-004  
**Version:** 1.5  
**Target:** Home Chef Senior Hosts, Kitchen Audit Officers

---

## 1. Safety & Hygiene Charter

All home-cooked meals prepared under Saarthi Kitchen must adhere to strict FSSAI home kitchen guidelines to guarantee student health and nutritional quality.

---

## 2. Kitchen Audit Checklist (Pre-Listing Inspection)

- [ ] **Potable Water Source:** RO water filtration system installed and serviced within last 6 months.
- [ ] **Food Storage & Refrigeration:** Clean, segregated vegetarian storage at ≤ 4°C.
- [ ] **Packaging Standards:** 100% food-grade, biodegradable sugarcane-bagasse or tamper-sealed reusable tiffin boxes.
- [ ] **Oil & Spice Standards:** Only cold-pressed mustard oil, sunflower oil, or pure cow ghee. Zero industrial palm oil or artificial food coloring allowed.

---

## 3. Daily Dispatch Protocol

1. **Cooking Window:** Meals must be cooked within 90 minutes of delivery.
2. **Thermal Bag Transit:** Tiffins placed in insulated thermal bags to maintain > 65°C core temperature until gate handover.
3. **Weekly Tuesday Host Settlement:** Host receives ₹55 per tiffin directly via UPI every Tuesday.

---

## 4. Student Feedback & Quality Rating Thresholds

- Any host rating dropping below **4.3 / 5.0** over 7 consecutive days triggers an unannounced quality check.
- 100% refund offered to the student on any verified meal discrepancy.
